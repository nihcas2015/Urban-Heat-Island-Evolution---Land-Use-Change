Core Finding

Built-up index (NDBI) contributes +23.5°C per unit increase — over 4× stronger than vegetative cooling (-5.7°C), demonstrating that built land expansion is the primary driver of regional heat anomalies.

⚠️
Spatial Scale Resolution

Averaging across administrative polygons smooths micro-climatic gradients. Regression models capture broad macro-level structural trends rather than localized street-level canyon heating.
