Methodology

Analysis Pipeline

A sequential spatial mining approach applied to multi-year satellite composites over 200 Chennai wards.

🛰️

Data Acquisition

Sentinel-2 SR (2016–2024) for NDVI & NDBI. Landsat-8/9 Collection 2 for LST. Annual cloud-free median composites via Google Earth Engine.

Spatial Aggregation

200 actual Chennai ward polygons. Mean indicator values extracted per ward using reduceRegions() in GEE. Results joined with ward metadata.

Mann-Kendall Test

Non-parametric monotonic trend detection (pymannkendall) per ward LST series. Classifies each ward as increasing / decreasing / no trend at α = 0.05.

🔍

PELT Change-point

Pruned Exact Linear Time algorithm (ruptures) detects structural breaks — years of sudden LST regime shift within a ward, typically from rapid land-use change.

rupturesPELTchange-point
→
📊

OLS Regression

LST regressed on NDVI, NDBI, and rainfall across all ward-years. Reveals relative contribution of vegetation loss vs. built-up expansion to heat intensity.

scikit-learnOLSR² = 0.17
🔬
Key Finding

NDBI contributes +23.5°C per unit — over 4× stronger than NDVI's cooling effect (+5.7°C). Urban expansion outpaces vegetation-based heat mitigation in Chennai.

⚠️
Scale Note

R² = 0.17 at ward scale is expected — ward-mean averaging smooths sub-ward micro-climate gradients. The regression captures the broad structural signal, not local hotspots.
